Safety & Privacy

How your data is protected

Anonymous by Default

Your complaints are submitted without any personally identifiable information. Only a device-based pseudonymous ID is generated.

Tamper-Proof Logging

Every action is recorded in a cryptographic hash chain. Any modification is immediately detectable, ensuring accountability.

Auto-Escalation

Complaints ignored for more than 3 days are automatically escalated and flagged publicly, ensuring no grievance is buried.

Community Verification

Community members validate complaints through upvotes, building a weighted trust score that reflects collective credibility.

AI-Assisted Processing

Voice recordings and text are auto-summarized and categorized using AI, making processing faster for officials.

Identity Masking

Public dashboards display only masked identifiers. No real names, phone numbers, or locations are ever exposed.
